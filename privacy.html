Privacy

The short version.

Pegasus runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Nothing you do here ever leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Pegasus has no backend and no login. Your progress, streak, personal images, and settings are saved only in your own browser, using localStorage on the device you are using.

No tracking, no analytics

We do not use Google Analytics or any other analytics or tracking tool. There are no servers behind the app, so we have no way to see how you use it. We cannot see your answers, your scores, your images, or anything else.

No accounts

There is no sign in and no sign up. We never ask for your email, your name, or any personal information.

No ads

Pegasus has no ads and never will. Nobody pays to put anything in front of you.

Your data is yours

Because everything lives in your browser, you are in full control. Open Settings and tap Reset to wipe it all instantly, or clear your browser's site data. Since the data lives only on one device, clearing your browser or moving to another device means you start fresh.

Open source

Pegasus is a single open source file under the MIT License. You can read every line and confirm for yourself that it does nothing more than run on your device.

Contact

For privacy questions, feedback, or anything else, send a message on LinkedIn.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, we will update this page and note the change at the bottom.
